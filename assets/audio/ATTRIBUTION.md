# Forest Chopper — audio sources, changes and licenses

The audio files in this directory are **converted adaptations**, provided locally without a third-party runtime dependency. All samples were compressed to mono MP3 at 24 kHz (48–56 kb/s), and some were trimmed, layered, speed-adjusted or loop-crossfaded. Preserve this file when sharing or distributing the game.

Chop sound cut boundaries are in [`CHOP_CUTS.json`](metadata/CHOP_CUTS.json), generated by [`scripts/prepare_chopping_sounds.py`](../../scripts/prepare_chopping_sounds.py). The owl sample was deleted entirely; nighttime uses crickets and the general ambience instead.

The earlier male and female pain selections were replaced. Only the approved female fragments (pain: 3, 4, 5, 20; jumps: 24–28) are included; 28 rejected takes were deleted. IDs and source timecodes are in [`VOICE_CUTS.json`](metadata/VOICE_CUTS.json). To reproduce these voice clips from the original downloaded assets use [`scripts/prepare_voice_sounds.py`](../../scripts/prepare_voice_sounds.py).
